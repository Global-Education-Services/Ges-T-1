SUMMARY:
Global Education Services L.L.C. “GES Quality Education” 
a leading private education group of K-12 premium 
schools, preschools, and early learning centres. 
Founded in 2012, GES Quality Education was born to 
be the fastest internationally recognized Omani 
premier private education group and behind digital 
reinvention that is based on technology, knowledge 
and innovation drawing upon Oman’s stability to 
attract, develop and retain talent from the region and 
beyond. Over the years, GES’s team of top tier talented 
experts with extensive experience focused on 
opening new schools, turning around failing schools 
and growing, and improving existing ones. 2022 marks 
the 10th year since establishment and maintains a 
commitment to continuous improvement on productive 
learning environments, equitable resource allocation 
that meets the needs of learners, and effective 
leadership. GES has also successfully achieved and 
attained international accreditation through Cognia 
by embarking on a long-term continuous process for 
improvement.
STRATEGIC APPROACH:
Our strategic approach aims at building relationships, 
partnerships, integration, and generating educational 
development opportunities. As the leading educational 
provider in Oman, GES has established strategic 
partnerships with leading international educational 
institutions to increase expertise, resources and reach 
new markets. GES is committed to being a pioneer 
and leader in education and training innovation. The 
company aims to continue building links with local 
and international institutions and increase perspective 
and understanding of educational innovation 
initiatives. As the company continues to build its 
portfolio and operational development within the 
region, it continuously seeks to provide investment 
and knowledge-sharing opportunities to their 
partners and potential investors.
LEADERS IN THE EDUCATION SECTOR:
The corporation is highly regarded as the leading 
educational provider in Oman. A key leadership goal is 
to develop outstanding leaders within the organization 
to achieve outcomes that transcend everyday organizational 
imperatives. Our main priority is to inspire a positive 
operational culture and set a clear pathway through 
growth initiatives. We work extensively with our 
partners to nurture growth in various business areas 
and expand our operations internationally. 
INNOVATION: 
GES recognizes the value in preparing and upskilling 
young learners for the new data-driven economy. In 
the current economic climate and after the global 
pandemic crisis, new learning opportunities present 
itself as we review what today’s young learners will 
need to be successful. To empower the next generation 
of inventive problem solvers, a primary objective is to 
train students in innovative thinking, entrepreneurship, 
creativity, and problem thinking. Our aim is to create 
an environment that encourages and guides students 
to develop a creative mindset. In pursuit of this objective 
GES has partnered with a leading educational institution 
to establish an innovation and research hub in the 
heart of Muscat. The innovation and research hub 
seeks to provide students with access to 
cutting-edge technological laboratories and foster a 
spirit of imagination and collaboration amongst 
young people. 
OMAN’S VISION 2040
GES has made significant progress to achieve strategic 
educational goals to serve Oman’s future generation. 
A key aspect of its strategy is to provide young 
learners with the necessary practical and technical 
skills to become more independent thinkers and 
leaders. Several key initiatives and projects were 
successfully implemented, as recently Shomoukh 
Early Years Education state of the art building was 
officially launched in January 2022. The concept and 
design of the educational building is to inspire and 
encourage sustainable thinking and reduce its energy 
needs. A key element of the building design concept is 
to provide an outstanding teaching and learning 
environment for all learners and accommodate a 
range of learning techniques.

Al Shomoukh International Private School
Al Shomoukh International Private School is a homegrown educational brand developed by Global Educa-
tion Services Company (GES) to provide premium education following the UK National Curriculum for 
Omani and international students. Launched in 2015, Al Shomoukh International Private School offers the 
highest quality international education from Kindergarten to Grade 12, welcoming students from all over the 
world.
Al Shomoukh International Private School follows the UK National Curriculum and is an approved Edexcel 
examinations centre. Parallel with this, the school ensures compliance with all Omani Ministry of Education 
requirements and prepares students for the UK Edexcel IGCSE and A-levels examinations.
